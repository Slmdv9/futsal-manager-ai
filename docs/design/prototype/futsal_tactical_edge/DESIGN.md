## Brand & Style

The design system projects elite athletic performance, surgical tactical precision, and high-tier athletic intellect. Engineered specifically for tactical decision-makers, managers, and data analysts in elite futsal, the aesthetic balances modern sports minimalism with data-dense clarity.

The visual language communicates authority and rapid legibility under fast-paced pitch conditions:
- **Clean Tactical Minimalism:** Stripped of unnecessary decoration to prioritize pitch boards, session management, kinetic movements, and biometric analysis.
- **Sport-Performance Contrast:** Crisp, optical separation between clean light backdrops and deep graphite tactical marks, mimicking physical strategy clipboards elevated by intelligent digital instrumentation.
- **Tactical Micro-Accents:** Strategic use of pitch emerald and tactical cyan signals actionable insight, player vector tracks, set-piece triggers, and computational suggestions without cognitive overload.

## Colors

The color palette is built exclusively around a high-clarity Light Mode architecture. It maximizes indoor sports arena legibility while maintaining the polished, refined tone of professional analytics software:

- **Primary (`#059669` / Pitch Emerald):** Represents foundational tactical execution, positive progression, tactical success rates, and primary calls to action.
- **Secondary (`#0284c7` / Vector Cyan):** Directs tactical paths, spatial zones, set-piece line markers, defensive transitions, and machine-learning suggestions.
- **Tertiary (`#10b981` / Active Turf):** High-energy feedback state for real-time play recognition, active substitutions, stamina metrics, and quick-filter states.
- **Neutral Core (`#0f172a` & `#1e293b` / Carbon & Dark Graphite):** Anchors typographic hierarchy, pitch boundaries, and primary structural headers with razor-sharp edge contrast.
- **Foundation Planes (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`, `#f1f5f9`):** Pure white elevates focused modules and analytical cards against subtle cooling slate backdrops, ensuring visual depth without muddy gray tones.
- **Structural Outlines (`#e2e8f0`):** Defines clean boundary containment without adding heavy optical weight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples geometric athletic structure with technical analytical discipline:

- **Headings (Space Grotesk):** Provides a muscular, contemporary sports cadence. Tight letter-spacing and strong vertical strokes grant match boards, play titles, and team sections immediate tactical punch.
- **Body Text (Hanken Grotesk):** Provides neutral, low-strain reading across play notes, tactical advice logs, training session descriptions, and roster listings.
- **Data & Numerical Metrics (JetBrains Mono):** Dedicated to game clocks, pitch coordinates, player numbers, tactical metrics (xG, high-press intensity, defensive recovery times), and technical state indicators to enforce precision layout alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed around an 8pt dynamic spatial grid that maintains rigorous structural order across handheld tablets, phones, and expansive desktop tactical centers:

- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, `1rem` margin, `1rem` gutter. Bottom tactical tool dock and compact vertical card stacking.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with split-pane mode (interactive pitch schematic occupying 55% viewport, tactical parameter cards occupying 45%). `2rem` outer margins.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with a fixed secondary workbench rail (`320px`), central fluid pitch monitor, and collapsible tactical metrics inspector on the trailing margin.

Pitch board elements must maintain an immutable aspect ratio corresponding to official futsal regulation dimensions (2:1 proportion: 40m x 20m), regardless of viewport adjustments.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through high-precision tactile layers rather than heavy diffuse drop shadows, preserving an elite athletic notebook feel:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Surfaces rendered in `#f8fafc` create calm background continuity.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Shells):** `#ffffff` solid planes bounded by hairline `#e2e8f0` structural borders. Soft resting elevation: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Tactical Draggables & Interactive Panels):** Hover cards, player movement tags, and drill selectors employ an elevated crisp shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, bordered by `#cbd5e1`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Overlays & Pitch Strategy Boards):** Critical tactical drawers and playbook overlays utilize: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` over a subtle `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25)` backdrop blur (`4px`).

## Shapes

The interface adopts a calibrated roundedness level (`2`), evoking the industrial engineering of athletic tactical tablets while preserving contemporary SaaS approachability:

- Base cards, tactical metric tiles, and playbook blocks utilize standard corner radiuses (`0.5rem` / `8px`).
- Internal action chips, status badges, and player position nodes scale up to soft pills (`rounded-full`) to denote kinetic, touch-friendly interactability.
- Tactical pitch surfaces maintain crisp, distinct boundaries with an outer perimeter corner radius of `12px`, framing court zones with strict geometrical precision.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Tactical Action:** Background `#059669`, text `#ffffff`, font `Hanken Grotesk` medium (`14px`), border-radius `0.5rem`. States: hover `#047857`, active `#065f46`, focus outline `2px solid #10b981` with `2px` offset.
- **Secondary Action (Play Engine):** Background `#ffffff`, text `#0f172a`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`. States: hover `#f8fafc`, active `#f1f5f9`.
- **AI Assist / Pitch Action:** Background `#0284c7`, text `#ffffff`. Provides high visual distinction for AI-generated playbook routes and formation recommendations.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags:** Compact typography (`JetBrains Mono`, `11px`). 
- **Roles & Zones:** Defending (`#f1f5f9` surface, `#1e293b` text), Press Trigger (`#ecfdf5` surface, `#059669` text, `#a7f3d0` border), Tactical Alert (`#f0f9ff` surface, `#0284c7` text, `#bae6fd` border).

### Lists & Strategy Rows
- Horizontal player rosters and set-piece libraries utilize alternating subtle hover states (`#f8fafc`).
- Dividing rules stick to single-pixel `#f1f5f9` rules with strict padding (`0.75rem` vertical, `1rem` horizontal) for rapid touch scanning.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, placeholder `#94a3b8`, text `#0f172a`.
- Focus state: border-color `#059669` accompanied by `0 0 0 3px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.12)`.

### Cards & Analytical Containers
- Pure white surfaces (`#ffffff`) framed by single-pixel `#e2e8f0` borders. Header zones cleanly separated by hairline dividing tracks with mono-spaced metric labels right-aligned.

### Domain-Specific: Interactive Pitch Board
- Court surface color `#f1f5f9` or `#ffffff` with high-contrast court boundaries (`#cbd5e1` markings).
- Player Tokens: Circular nodes (`36px`), filled with `#0f172a` (Home) and `#0284c7` (Away/Tactical variation), containing crisp high-contrast squad numbers in `JetBrains Mono`.
- Vector Movement Indicators: Dashed dynamic curves rendered in `#059669` with oriented arrow terminations indicating runs, press zones, and passing lines.
